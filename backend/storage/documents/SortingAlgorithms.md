# Sorting Algorithms

Sorting is a fundamental operation in computer science. Here are the most common algorithms:

## 1. Quick Sort
- **Strategy**: Divide and Conquer. Picks a 'pivot' and partitions the array.
- **Complexity**: Average $O(n \log n)$, Worst $O(n^2)$.
- **Space**: $O(\log n)$.

## 2. Merge Sort
- **Strategy**: Divide and Conquer. Recursively splits array and merges sorted halves.
- **Complexity**: Stable $O(n \log n)$ in all cases.
- **Space**: $O(n)$.

## 3. Bubble Sort
- **Strategy**: Repeatedly steps through the list, compares adjacent elements and swaps them.
- **Complexity**: $O(n^2)$.
